This Privacy Policy explains how NexaTrack (“NexaTrack,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use our website, shipment-tracking pages, customer support tools, dashboards, and related services (collectively, the “Services”).

This page is a general website privacy policy template. Before publishing it for a live business, replace the contact details and have the policy reviewed for the laws and services that apply to your operation.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the Services, we may collect:

  • Contact information: name, email address, company, phone number, and information you submit through contact or support forms.
  • Shipment information: tracking numbers, shipment status, origin and destination information, delivery details, carrier information, and other logistics data needed to provide tracking.
  • Account and dashboard information: login or account details and preferences, where account features are enabled.
  • Device and usage information: IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, approximate location derived from IP, timestamps, and similar technical data.
  • Support communications: messages and information you provide when contacting customer support, including through third-party chat services if enabled.

2. How we use information

We may use information to provide and operate shipment tracking, display logistics data, respond to requests, maintain dashboards, communicate with customers, troubleshoot technical issues, improve the Services, prevent fraud or abuse, protect our systems, and comply with legal obligations.

3. When we share information

We may share information with service providers that help us operate the Services, such as hosting, analytics, communications, customer support, payment, security, and logistics technology providers. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transfer such as a merger or acquisition.

We do not treat your shipment tracking number or email address as public information merely because you submit it to our tracking form.

4. Cookies & analytics

NexaTrack may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to remember preferences, support website functionality, understand usage, and improve performance. Some third-party services may also set their own cookies or similar technologies.

If live customer support is enabled through tawk.to, that service may process information about your chat session, device, and interactions according to its own privacy practices. Review the applicable third-party privacy notice before enabling the service in production.

5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, maintain business and shipment records, resolve disputes, meet legal requirements, and enforce our agreements. Retention periods may vary by the type of information and the purpose for which it was collected.

7. Your choices & rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection to certain processing. You may also be able to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

8. Children's privacy

The Services are intended for business and general users and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where prohibited by applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Material changes may also be communicated through the Services when appropriate.
